03 · featured build

sales data analysis & automated dashboards

problem Raw sales data is hard to read and slow to report on.

what i did Cleaned the data, analysed trends, and built automated dashboards in Excel and Power BI.

what i found Built an executive dashboard to track revenue, profit, units sold, orders, and gross profit margin. It compares monthly performance, sales channels, regions, and leading countries, with filters for product category, country, and quarter. The dashboard shows $645.2K revenue, $296.4K total profit, a 46% gross profit margin, 6,698 units sold, and 2,707 orders and $348.9K COGS.
